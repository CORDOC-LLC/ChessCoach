## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Play chess against a real Stockfish engine, right on your device -- no account,
no ads, no internet required to play.

CHESSCOACH GIVES YOU:

- A real opponent. Play against Stockfish at any strength from beginner to
  master, fully on-device.
- Instant move grading. Every move you play is classified -- Best, Excellent,
  Good, Inaccuracy, Mistake, Blunder -- so you always know how you're doing.
- Hints when you want them. Stuck on a position? Get the engine's top move
  and a plain-language reason why.
- Full game review. After every game, revisit it move by move: a win%
  graph across the whole game, your accuracy score, and every mistake
  flagged with what you should have played instead.
- Resume anytime. Close the app mid-game and pick up exactly where you left
  off.
- Lessons. Short explanations of core tactical ideas -- forks, pins,
  skewers, discovered attacks, and more -- each paired with real practice
  puzzles.
- Puzzles. Hundreds of rated tactical puzzles across a dozen themes, from
  beginner to master strength.
- Openings. Browse and step through named opening lines to build your
  repertoire.

Your games stay on your device. ChessCoach doesn't require an account and
doesn't upload your games anywhere.

A one-time Review Plan purchase unlocks full move-by-move analysis for
every game, beyond the free tier -- pay once, own it forever, no
subscription.
